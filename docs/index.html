Price Paid Data and the new UPRN look-up

On 28 August 2026 HM Land Registry published a stable property identifier for Price Paid Data for the first time. This is the working behind an analysis of what it can and cannot match — the tables, the assertions, and the reasoning, all reproducible from the raw files.

Start here

  • Figures Four charts with data tables — unmatched share by property type, the new-build comparison, the stratified view, and the risk ratios on a log scale. Generated from the Gold tables, never hand-edited.
  • The write-up The article on Medium: what the look-up misses, why the obvious explanation turned out to be wrong twice, and what a stable key made possible.
  • Findings Every result with its confidence interval, the effect sizes, the corrections I made along the way, and what the data cannot support.
  • Data dictionary The Silver tables, their grain, why the fact table carries no UPRN column, and the assertions that run on every build.
  • Sources and field specs The files ingested, the field order, the specification's exclusion categories, licensing, and the reconciliation endpoint.
  • The repository Bronze, Silver and Gold in pandas and Parquet, with the validation gate and the test suite. Clone it and the numbers reproduce, or a test tells you why not.

No official match rate has been published for the look-up. Every figure in this analysis is computed from the raw files by the code in the repository, and reconciled against HM Land Registry's own transaction count through a separate publication channel.

This page is a table of contents and deliberately quotes no figures — the numbers live in the generated pages and the Gold tables, where they cannot drift.
